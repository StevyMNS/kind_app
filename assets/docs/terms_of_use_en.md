# Terms of Use

**Last Updated:** April 2026

By using the **One Kind Message** application, you accept these Terms of Use. Our goal is to create a healthy, positive, and kind digital space. Certain rules are essential to preserve this purpose.

## 1. Application Purpose

One Kind Message's sole purpose is the propagation of kind messages (encouragements, prayers, positive thoughts, verses). Any use contrary to this spirit is strictly forbidden.

## 2. Prohibited Content

To maintain a "Digital Sanctuary," you are **strictly forbidden** to send:
- Hateful, racist, homophobic, or discriminatory messages.
- Insults, obscenities, or violent language.
- Advertising, spam, or external links.
- Any personally identifiable information (yours or a third party's).

## 3. Daily Limits

The application is deliberately restricted to:
- **One message sent per day.**
- **One message received per day.**
This limit is designed to bring value back to words and foster a contemplative experience. Attempts to bypass this limit violate these terms.

## 4. Anonymity and Responsibility

Although One Kind Message does not ask for personal information and operates anonymously, the content you write is solely your responsibility. If abusive behavior linked to a device identifier is detected, access to the service may be blocked.

## 5. Application Changes

We reserve the right to modify, suspend, or discontinue the application at any time without notice, with no obligation to ensure continuous service, as the app operates on the principle of giving and kindness.

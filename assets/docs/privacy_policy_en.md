# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** April 2026

Welcome to **One Kind Message**. Protecting your privacy and personal data is at the heart of our concept. Our application is built on a fundamental principle: **complete anonymity**.

## 1. Data Collected

One Kind Message does not collect **any personally identifiable information** about you.
- **No user accounts**: Authentication is entirely silent and anonymous.
- **No email addresses or phone numbers**: We do not ask for any contact details.
- **No ad tracking**: We do not use advertising trackers or third-party targeting cookies.

Here is the only data we record:
- The content of the messages you send, in an unmodifiable manner.
- The date and time the message was sent.
- Your anonymously generated device identifier (UUID) to maintain message history and daily limits.

## 2. Data Usage

The collected data is used exclusively to:
- Deliver your message to an anonymous recipient.
- Display your personal history of sent and received messages.
- Protect the application against abuse (enforcing the one message per day limit).

## 3. Data Sharing

**No third party will ever have access to your information.**
We do not sell, rent, or transfer any of your data (anonymous or otherwise) to third-party companies, agencies, or services.

## 4. Security

Your messages are securely stored on our servers. Access to the database is protected by strict protocols (Row Level Security via Supabase), ensuring that only the sender and the designated recipient can read a message.

## 5. Contact

If you have questions about this policy, please note that we do not have any information about you. However, feel free to consult our open-source repositories or contact the community.
